# The two minute script

Everything you say, in order, with what you do beside it. Read it straight
down. Nothing here is faked.

There are only two kinds of line:

- **SAY** means say it out loud with the key held down. That is you, the
  twelve year old, talking to the person at the door.
- **VOICE** means narration for the video. Record it separately afterwards
  and lay it over the top. Do not speak narration into the game.

Record on the live link: **https://coverstory-eta.vercel.app/play**

Headphones on. If the grown up's voice reaches your microphone the game
scores it as you talking.

---

## 0:00  Cover

Do nothing. Let the opening screen sit for six seconds. The shadows under
the door shift once. That is the shot.

No narration. Silence is the point.

---

## 0:06  Prep

**Click** `Get your story straight`.

**Type into slot 7:**

> He's in the shower, can you come back?

**Click** `Mom` on that row.

**Click** `Let them knock`.

No narration. Ten seconds, and the viewer has seen that you can write lines
in advance.

---

## 0:16  The first answer

The driver knocks and speaks. **Wait until he has completely finished.**

**Hold `1`.** Keep it held.

**SAY:**

> Yeah, just leave it on the step, mate.

**Release `1`.** Wait. Dad's voice says it back.

**No narration over this.** This is the whole product in one gesture and
talking across it spends it.

---

## 0:34  The only explanation in the video

**VOICE:**

> You are twelve. You are home alone. Four people are going to knock
> tonight, and none of them will talk to a child.

Nothing else. No feature list.

---

## 0:42  The shout

Your second answer, not your first. The game needs to have heard your normal
voice once before it can tell that you raised it.

**Hold `1`.**

**SAY,** and genuinely raise your voice on the last word:

> He's in the shower, come back LATER.

**Release.** Dad says it back heavier and louder.

**VOICE:**

> It heard me shout, so the grown up shouts.

---

## 1:00  The meter

Get through the first door. The neighbour knocks by herself. Wait for her to
ask for someone by name.

**Hold `3`,** which is Uncle, answering a question about Mom. This is
deliberately the wrong one.

**SAY:**

> She's upstairs, I'll get her.

**Release.** The light widens and **Wrong voice +18** appears.

**VOICE:**

> Scored in code, so it can always tell me what I did.

---

## 1:15  The one that wins it

You need to have already told the driver that Dad is in the garage. If you
did not, do it at the next question and spring this at the door after.

**Hold `1`.**

**SAY:**

> Dad's at work till nine.

**Release.** She catches it:

> But you said he was in the garage?

**Doesn't add up +26.** The light goes red.

**VOICE:**

> It is not judging whether that sounded suspicious. It is checking one
> answer against everything the house has said all night.

Make the clash obvious. Garage against work is clean. Busy against around is
not, and will not be caught.

---

## 1:45  The prepared line

At the next question, **tap `7`** and say nothing at all.

Mom's voice: *"He's in the shower, can you come back?"*

**VOICE:**

> Written before I knew the question, so it buys a moment, not an answer.

---

## 1:55  The tape

Let the night finish. The end screen comes up on its own with the replay
already on it.

**Click** `Play it back`. Let it run four seconds.

**Click** `Download the tape`.

**VOICE:**

> Every line of the night, both voices, with the word timings the speech API
> returned.

---

## 2:05  Close

**VOICE:**

> Speech to text hears me. The LLM Gateway writes the visitor and judges
> whether I contradicted myself. Text to speech is the whole cast.

Hold on the link.

---

## If you get caught early, keep it

A night that ends in being caught is a better video than a clean one. It
proves the game can be lost, which is the only thing that makes surviving it
worth anything. The end screen says how many doors you got through, so the
story still lands.

## If a line is not caught

Say it blunter and go again. Three attempts at a beat is normal.

## Watch your run count

The live link allows five nights an hour. Rehearse on localhost, record on
the link.
