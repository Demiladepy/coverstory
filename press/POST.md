## As a thread, if you want to show the engineering

**1/**

> You're 12. You're home alone. Someone's at the door and they want an adult.
>
> So you become one.
>
> Cover Story is a voice game built on @AssemblyAI. Hold a key, talk, and the
> house answers in a grown-up's voice.
>
> coverstory-eta.vercel.app/play

**2/**

> The part I did not expect to work: it hears you shout.
>
> Your raised voice is measured against your own normal speaking level, not a
> fixed threshold. Push a word past the line and the grown-up says that word
> louder too. Nobody tells you this. You just find it.

**3/**

> The visitor and the judge are two separate model calls.
>
> One is a character, written hot, and it is allowed to be unreasonable. The
> other is cold, has no persona, and is asked one question: does this clash
> with anything the house has said tonight?
>
> Combined, it hallucinated contradictions every run.

**4/**

> So what you told the pizza driver at eight is still on the record when the
> neighbour knocks at nine.
>
> Say Dad is in the garage, then say he is at work, and she will ask you
> which.
>
> That is the whole game. Four doors, one night, one story to keep straight.

**5/**

> Streaming speech to text hears me.
> The LLM Gateway writes the visitor and judges the contradiction.
> Text to speech is the entire cast.
>
> Measured end to end: 315ms to finalise, 270ms to the first adult syllable.
>
> coverstory-eta.vercel.app/play
